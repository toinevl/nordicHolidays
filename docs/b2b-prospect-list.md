## Outreach Strategy

1. **Start with Tier 1** — these companies have the most direct value proposition. Reach out via LinkedIn or email with a personalized message referencing their specific Nordic product page.
2. **Offer free 3-month pilot** — embed the widget at no cost, no commitment. The goal is testimonials and usage data.
3. **Pilot pricing: EUR 49/month for 3 months**, then EUR 99–149/month. For the first 3 partners, waive the pilot fee entirely.
4. **Key metric**: does the widget increase time-on-site or generate leads? Track affiliate click-throughs (#74 beacon) and lead captures (#76).
5. **Pivot gate**: after 30 conversations with 0 pilots (month 4–5), reconsider whether B2B widget licensing is the right monetization path.
