About Fjordvia

Fjordvia is a small, independent travel service from the Netherlands for trips to Scandinavia and the wider Nordics. We design ready-to-drive itineraries — routes, overnight stays, food stops and activities — and offer partners a white-label trip-planner widget for their own websites. Fjordvia is a one-person business based in the Netherlands.
